# Product Requirements Document (PRD) for AMI by Arham

## 1. Introduction
AMI by Arham is a digital-first platform designed to provide a premium, bespoke jewelry experience focusing on gold and lab-grown diamonds. The platform serves as a lead-generation and discovery engine, eschewing traditional e-commerce catalogs in favor of personalized curation. All visual direction, typography, and brand aesthetics must adhere strictly to the provided `design.md` guidelines and incorporate the official brand logo.

## 2. Goals and Objectives
*   **Brand Authority:** Establish a "Modern Royal" aesthetic through high-end storytelling and visual presentation.
*   **Frictionless Lead Capture:** Convert high-intent visitors into custom bespoke requests via two primary funnels.
*   **Inspiration Discovery:** Gamify the jewelry discovery process to assist users in identifying their preferred aesthetic without feeling overwhelmed.

## 3. Target Audience
*   **The Heirloom Buyer:** High-intent customers seeking massive, royal-aesthetic lab-grown diamond pieces.
*   **The Visionary:** Customers who already have a specific design in mind (e.g., from an Instagram Reel or Pinterest board) and require a trusted jeweler to craft it.

## 4. Core Features
### 4.1. Story-Driven Landing Page
*   Immersive, scroll-triggered animations conveying the brand’s heritage and commitment to craftsmanship.
*   Prominent display of the brand logo and adherence to the color palette specified in `design.md`.
*   Clear dual Calls-to-Action (CTAs): "Submit Your Vision" and "Discover Inspiration".

### 4.2. "Bring Your Own Idea" Portal
*   Input fields supporting external URLs (Pinterest, Instagram).
*   Direct media upload support for reference images.
*   Text area for custom modification requests (e.g., "Change to 18k rose gold").
*   Lead capture form (Name, Email, WhatsApp number).

### 4.3. Inspiration Swipe Engine
*   A fluid, Tinder-style swipe interface presenting curated brand images.
*   Right-swipe saves the image to a user's "Favorites Board".
*   Left-swipe discards the image.

### 4.4. Favorites Board & Quote Request
*   A persistent view of all right-swiped items.
*   Single-click submission of the curated board to the administration team for a custom quote, alongside user contact details.

## 5. User Stories
*   *As a user with a specific design,* I want to paste a link from Pinterest so that the jeweler can recreate my vision.
*   *As a user seeking ideas,* I want to swipe through premium designs and save my favorites so that I can easily request a quote for the styles I like.
*   *As a brand administrator,* I want to receive organized submissions containing user contact info and their design references so that I can promptly follow up via WhatsApp.
